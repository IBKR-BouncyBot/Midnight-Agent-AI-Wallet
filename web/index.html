FROM POLICY TO PAYMENT

A small allowance. A clear boundary.

  1. 1

    Set the policy.

    The owner chooses an agent, approved recipients, a payment ceiling and a total allowance.

  2. 2

    Fund the vault.

    A shielded coin enters contract custody. The owner keeps a separate recovery authority.

  3. 3

    Prove permission.

    A permitted agent payment spends from the vault and updates its private accounting together.

  4. 4

    Check settlement.

    Confirm the network result and recipient receipt. Qualify the remaining change before spending again.

KNOW WHAT YOU ARE TESTING

Research, with receipts.

The earlier local runner verified real payments on public Midnight Preview, including recipient receipt and vault change. The new browser and Lace flow has its own verification record. A connected wallet or generated proof alone is never a completed payment.

This prototype has not been audited. Private amounts and policy openings do not hide every public detail: contract calls, timing, commitments and request nullifiers remain visible. Use disposable test assets.
